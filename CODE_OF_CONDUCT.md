# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Use welcoming language, accept constructive feedback, focus on community benefit, and show empathy. Harassment, trolling, personal attacks, and publishing private information are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove contributions or ban participants for inappropriate conduct. Report incidents privately through repository security contacts. Enforcement decisions will be fair and proportionate.

Adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at <https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html>.
